Privacy Policy for Zewari

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Zewari operates the Zewari customer app and the Zewari Courier app for delivery ordering and courier delivery.

Contact: haylemikaeltefera@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Account and sign-in information (Google or Apple)
  • Pickup and drop-off addresses and map locations
  • Receiver phone number (customer app)
  • Order and delivery details and history
  • Courier location while available / on a delivery (courier app, with permission)
  • Device push notification token
  • Crash and diagnostic information

With your permission, we access device location for address entry (customer) or delivery tracking (courier).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process, assign, and track delivery orders
  • Show maps and location features
  • Send order and delivery notifications
  • Improve app stability and fix crashes

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We may share information with service providers (for example Google Maps, Firebase, Supabase, and our hosting provider), customers or couriers as needed to complete deliveries, and authorities if required by law.

4. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service, keep order records, and meet legal requirements.

5. Your Choices

Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing haylemikaeltefera@gmail.com, or use our account deletion page.
