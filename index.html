Zewari – Request Account Deletion

This page is for users of the Zewari customer app who want to delete their account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us at YOUR_EMAIL@example.com
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Zewari account
  3. Include the phone number or email used to sign in (Google or Apple)

We will process your request within 30 days.

Data we delete

  • Name, email, phone number, and account profile
  • Saved addresses and location data tied to your account
  • Push notification tokens
  • Sign-in / account identifiers we store

Data we may keep

  • Order history needed for legal, fraud, or accounting reasons — for up to 90 days, then deleted or anonymized
